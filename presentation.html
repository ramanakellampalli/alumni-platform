Core Features
Everything in One Place
Six feature areas accessible via tabs — each serving a distinct need for the reunion.
📅

Meetings

Schedule & join virtual meetings. Live "Join Now" button activates automatically when a meeting starts. Committee-based access control.

💰

Donations

View every donation — donor name, amount, year of study, village. Paginated for easy browsing. Fully transparent to all members.

📄

Expenses

Track every rupee spent — category, description, date, who recorded it. Keeps the community confident in fund management.

👥

Committees

Browse all sub-committees and their members. Each alumnus belongs to one committee and can see fellow members' details.

📊

Reports

Auto-generated financial summary — total raised, total spent, net balance. Visual bar charts. Downloadable at any time.

🏦

Banking

Official bank account details + a live QR code that alumni can scan with any UPI app to donate instantly.

Administration
How Admins Manage the Platform
Two admin tiers — Regular Admins for day-to-day operations, Super Admins for full control.
🛡️

Regular Admin

Day-to-day manager

Can schedule meetings, record donations and expenses, manage committees, add/remove members, and view all reports and banking information.

👑

Super Admin

Full platform control

Everything a Regular Admin can do, plus: create/remove admin accounts, bulk-import alumni data, and access the Operations tab for platform-wide management.

📅

Schedule Meetings

Create meetings with date, time range, Zoom link & committee access

💳

Record Funds

Log incoming donations and outgoing expenses with full details

👤

Manage Members

Add, edit, assign to committees, or remove alumni accounts

📥

Bulk Import

Upload CSV to register many alumni at once (Super Admin only)
